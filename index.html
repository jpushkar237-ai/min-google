💡 AI Lab Takeaways (Why Google Won in 1998)

  • The 1997 Problem (Keyword Stuffing): Search pizza or cricket. The spam pages (pizza-pizza-pizza.com and cricket-cricket-cricket.com) repeat the keyword 10 times to rig the old ranking and take the #1 spot.
  • The 1998 Solution (Random Surfer): Because nobody links to the spam pages (Links in: 0), a random web surfer almost never lands on them (~0.8% score, purely from random teleportation). Meanwhile, legitimate websites with high incoming backlinks like best-pizza-pune.com rise to the top!
